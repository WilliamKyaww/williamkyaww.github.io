Privacy policy

Relay · effective 13 November 2026

Relay is a personal desktop app for Windows, built and used by its developer. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what Relay accesses and what it does with it.

The short version

  • Relay runs only on your own computer. There is no Relay server.
  • Nothing Relay reads is sent to the developer or to anyone else.
  • Nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Google user data (Gmail)

When you connect a Gmail account, Relay asks Google for permission to read, organise and send email on your behalf (the gmail.modify scope). It cannot permanently delete email.

  • Use. Relay uses this access only to show your email in the app, and to carry out what you ask: search, reply, send, mark as read, star and archive.
  • Storage. Email Relay has fetched is kept in a local database on your computer. The sign-in token Google issues is encrypted with Windows' own data protection. It is never shown in the interface or written to logs.
  • Sharing. Your Google data is not transferred to any server or third party. Humans do not read it, apart from you.
  • AI. Relay does not send your email to any AI service, and Google user data is never used to develop, improve or train AI or machine-learning models. If an optional assistant is added later, it will be off by default and ask before reading any account, and this policy will be updated before it ships.

Relay's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Microsoft accounts (Outlook)

If you connect an Outlook account, the same rules apply to the mail Relay reads through Microsoft Graph. It is shown, stored and acted on locally, at your request only, and never shared.

Other services

Services such as WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, Discord and LinkedIn run inside Relay as their own websites, loaded directly from those companies. Their own privacy policies apply. Relay keeps their sign-in sessions on your computer. If you turn on notification previews for a service, Relay keeps a local copy of each notification's title and text so it can show it in your inbox.

Removing your data

Disconnect an account in Relay, or uninstall Relay and delete its data folder, to remove everything it stored. You can also revoke Relay's Google access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Questions go to the developer through GitHub.
