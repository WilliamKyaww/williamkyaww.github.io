Relay

A personal, local-first messaging app for Windows.

Relay brings messages from several services into one calm place, so checking whether someone has been in touch doesn't mean opening feeds and recommendations. Email is read through each provider's official API. Other services run as their own web apps, held to their messaging pages.

Who it's for

Relay is a private project, built and used by its developer. It is not offered to the public, and no one else's account is connected to it.

How it treats your data

Everything stays on the computer it runs on. There is no Relay server, and nothing is sent to the developer. The privacy policy has the details.
